# Kid-Safe Apps Suite

A collection of five safe, educational, and fun mobile applications designed for children aged 8-12 with built-in parental controls and privacy features.

## 🎯 Project Overview

This project contains complete design documentation, UI specifications, and development roadmaps for five kid-friendly applications that promote financial literacy, creativity, responsibility, and entrepreneurial thinking—all in a safe, offline-first environment.

## 📱 The Apps

### 1. Cookie Tycoon Mini
**Type:** Casual Mobile Game
**Focus:** Business simulation, resource management

A cute bakery management game where kids bake cookies, upgrade tools, and sell products in fun mini-events. Completely offline with no chat features.

[📄 Full Design Doc](./docs/apps/cookie-tycoon-mini/DESIGN.md)

---

### 2. Kid-Safe Shop Tracker
**Type:** Productivity/Finance
**Focus:** Money management, goal setting

Helps kids track allowance, savings goals, and wishlist items with visual progress indicators and parent approval systems.

[📄 Full Design Doc](./docs/apps/kid-safe-shop-tracker/DESIGN.md)

---

### 3. Bake & Sell Helper
**Type:** Educational/Business Tools
**Focus:** Real-world entrepreneurship, math skills

A practical app for kids running their own cookie business, featuring recipe calculators, profit tracking, and sales analytics.

[📄 Full Design Doc](./docs/apps/bake-and-sell-helper/DESIGN.md)

---

### 4. Mini Missions App
**Type:** Gamified Chores/Task Manager
**Focus:** Responsibility, rewards system

A task management app where parents create missions with coin rewards, and kids complete them to earn digital and real-world rewards.

[📄 Full Design Doc](./docs/apps/mini-missions-app/DESIGN.md)

---

### 5. My Closet Builder
**Type:** Creative/Fashion Game
**Focus:** Style, planning, organization

A mix-and-match fashion game where kids create outfits, catalog their real wardrobe, and maintain a wishlist for new clothing items.

[📄 Full Design Doc](./docs/apps/my-closet-builder/DESIGN.md)

---

## 🛡️ Safety & Privacy Principles

All apps in this suite follow strict safety guidelines:

- ✅ **No social features or chat**
- ✅ **Offline-first design**
- ✅ **COPPA compliant**
- ✅ **Parental controls and approval systems**
- ✅ **No ads or in-app purchases without parent approval**
- ✅ **Age-appropriate content**
- ✅ **No data collection beyond device storage**
